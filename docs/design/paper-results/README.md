# Current experiment comparisons

The active paper uses [A+C300 on G10, CG10, and repository SS10](evidence10-AC300-20260909/README.md),
with 30 seeds per problem at classic uncertainty below 0.05 and matched SS10
continuations to 0.02 and 0.015.
The [comparison table](evidence10-AC300-20260909/COMPARISON.md) includes only completed cohorts.
The matched SS10 0.02 cohort also completed. The user cancelled the remaining
ladder on 2026-09-10; partial 0.01 checkpoints are preserved and 0.005 never
started. See [operational status](evidence10-AC300-20260909/OPERATIONS.md).
The installed paper includes the 0.05/0.02 paired phantom comparisons and the
[completed 0.015 classic follow-up](ss10-followup-20260911/README.md).
That follow-up also preserves the seven posterior-ESS continuations completed
before cancellation. No phantom-prefix reduction was performed at 0.015.

[Posterior recovery candidates, seed 0](posterior10/README.md) retain the Eggbox,
Rosenbrock, and Rastrigin ESS-target experiments and their full corner plots.
The exploratory SS10 seed-0 result there is reproduced exactly by the new baseline.
